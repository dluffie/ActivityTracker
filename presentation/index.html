Problem Statement

The Challenge Students Face

Under KTU regulations, every polytechnic student must earn activity points through extracurricular participation to graduate. Currently, this process is entirely manual.

📋

Paper-Based Records

Physical certificates and forms get lost, damaged, or misplaced

⏳

Slow Verification

Teachers must manually verify each certificate one by one

❌

No Transparency

Students have no way to track progress toward their required points

Problem Statement

Pain Points in the Current System

🔴

Last-minute panic

Students realize they haven't collected enough points only weeks before graduation

🔴

Inconsistent marking

Different teachers award different points for the same activities — no standard system

🔴

No central database

Every department maintains its own scattered records with no unified view

🔴

Administrative burden

Teachers spend hours manually computing point totals for reports and grade cards

Our Solution

A Smart, Digital
Activity Tracking System

A web application that digitizes the entire activity point lifecycle — from student submission to teacher verification to administrative reporting.

📱 Mobile-Friendly
🤖 AI-Powered Extraction
📊 Real-time Dashboards
📄 PDF Reports

Key Feature

🤖 AI-Powered Document Extraction

Students upload their activity certificates, and our Gemini AI automatically extracts:

  • ✅ Event name & organizer
  • ✅ Activity type & level
  • ✅ Date range & position achieved
  • ✅ Suggested activity points
⚠️ Safety: A confirmation popup warns students that AI can make mistakes — they must review before submitting.

Key Feature

🏫 Class Management & PDF Reports

Teachers subscribe to classes and can:

  • 📥 Download PDF reports with college header, student list, register numbers, and total activity points
  • 👁️ View individual student details with points breakdown charts
  • 🗑️ S6 (final year) data deletion — permanently remove graduated students with Cloudinary image cleanup
  • ⚠️ Double-confirmation required: type class name to confirm deletion
